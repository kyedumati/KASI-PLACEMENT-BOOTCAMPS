## How you are scored

The main measure is your improvement from the Day 1 diagnostic to the Day 5 post-assessment. The diagnostic itself is not graded.

| Component | Weight | When | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily lab submissions | 30% | Days 1 to 5 | Commits to your GitHub training repository |
| Post-assessment: coding | 30% | Day 5 | 3 problems on HackerRank or a similar platform |
| Post-assessment: MCQ | 20% | Day 5 | 30 questions across all modules |
| Daily recap quizzes | 10% | Days 2 to 5 | 10-question quiz |
| Capstone mini-project | 10% | Day 5 | Working FastAPI app in Docker with security defences |

**Completion certificate:** at least 60% overall and at least 90% attendance.

After the week, the Training and Placement cell sorts students into three tiers:

| Tier | Score | What follows |
|---|---|---|
| Placement-ready | 75% and above | Nominated for upcoming drives |
| Developing | 50% to 74% | The standard 30-day track |
| Needs support | Below 50% | The 30-day track plus 2 extra guided lab hours each week |

## Your repository

Create one GitHub repository named `placement-training-2026` on Day 1. Every lab and every evening problem goes into it:

```text
placement-training-2026/
├── README.md
├── day1/
├── day2/
└── day3/
```

Name each file after the problem, for example `day2/two_sum.py`.
